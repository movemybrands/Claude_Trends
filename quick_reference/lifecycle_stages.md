# TREND LIFECYCLE STAGES QUICK CHECK

Fast reference for identifying where trends are in their lifecycle

---

## STAGE OVERVIEW

| Stage | Awareness | Risk/Reward | Action |
|-------|-----------|-------------|--------|
| **Emerging** | 0-15% | High risk, high reward | Small bets, rapid experiments |
| **Accelerating** | 15-40% | Medium risk, high growth | Scale pilots, commit resources |
| **Mainstream** | 40-70% | Lower risk, crowded | Differentiation, efficiency |
| **Mature** | 70%+ | Low risk, low growth | Value optimization, adjacency |
| **Declining** | Negative trajectory | High risk, contrarian only | Exit or deep value play |

---

## STAGE 1: EMERGING (0-15% awareness)

**"Niche but growing"**

### Key Indicators

✅ **Google Trends:**
- Just starting to curve upward (vs. flat baseline)
- Search volume 5-10x lower than peak similar trends
- Related queries showing niche terminology

✅ **Social/Community:**
- Reddit: Discussions in niche subreddits only (<50K members)
- Twitter: <1K mentions/day, mostly from tech-forward accounts
- Discord/Slack: 1-2 communities discussing, <5K total members

✅ **Media Coverage:**
- Zero mainstream coverage
- Maybe 1-2 industry newsletter mentions
- Tech influencers starting to notice (<5 posts)

✅ **Products:**
- <10 SKUs available
- Mostly DTC, Kickstarter, or early-stage startup products
- Limited retail distribution (online only)

✅ **Geography:**
- 1 region, often 1-2 cities only
- Typically starts in tech hubs (SF, NY, London, Bangalore)

✅ **Customers:**
- Early adopters and innovators only
- Friends of founders, tech enthusiasts
- Willingness to tolerate bugs/incomplete features

### Strategic Implications

**Opportunity:**
- First-mover advantage possible
- Low competition, high optionality
- Can shape market definitions
- Cheap customer acquisition

**Risk:**
- May never break out (60%+ fail here)
- Limited data for validation
- Product-market fit uncertain
- Hard to forecast

**Investment Approach:**
- Small bets ($10K-$50K range)
- Rapid experimentation, fast iteration
- Low commitment, maintain optionality
- Portfolio approach (many small bets)

**Timing:**
- Entry point: NOW (if confident in signal)
- Expected duration in stage: 6-18 months
- Next stage catalyst: Mainstream media pickup, major funding, or 10x product availability

### Example Signals

**Positive (moving to Accelerating):**
- Search volume 3x in 30 days
- First mainstream media mention
- Series A funding announced
- Product availability expanding (5 → 15 SKUs)

**Negative (stalling or dying):**
- Flat search trends for 90+ days
- Community discussion declining
- No new products launching
- Founders pivoting away

## STAGE 2: ACCELERATING (15-40% awareness)

**"Breaking through"**

### Key Indicators

✅ **Google Trends:**
- Sharp upward trajectory (3-5x in 30 days)
- Crossing into mainstream search terms
- Related queries becoming less technical

✅ **Social/Community:**
- Reddit: Breaking out of niche subs into larger communities (r/technology, r/entrepreneur)
- Twitter: >5K mentions/day, trending occasionally
- Multi-platform spread (started Reddit, now on Twitter + LinkedIn + TikTok)

✅ **Media Coverage:**
- Mainstream outlets starting coverage (not just tech press)
- 5-10 articles/week from Tier-2 sources
- "What is X?" explainer articles appearing
- TV/podcast mentions beginning

✅ **Products:**
- 10-100 SKUs available
- Retail distribution beginning (Target, Best Buy pilots)
- 5-10 funded startups in space
- Major brands announcing "me too" products

✅ **Geography:**
- 2-3 regions showing independent signals
- Major metros in each region
- Cross-regional discussion ("X is huge in the US, starting in UK")

✅ **Customers:**
- Early majority starting to adopt
- "Pragmatists" comfortable trying
- Word-of-mouth spreading outside tech circles
- Corporate pilots beginning

### Strategic Implications

**Opportunity:**
- Strong growth phase, validated concept
- Still early enough for meaningful positioning
- Capital available (investors seeing traction)
- Talent wants to join

**Risk:**
- Competition entering rapidly
- Window closing (6-12 months to establish)
- Hype cycle beginning (risk of bubble)
- Customer expectations rising

**Investment Approach:**
- Scale validated experiments
- Commit significant resources ($500K-$5M)
- Move quickly, land-grab mentality
- Focus on distribution and brand

**Timing:**
- Entry point: URGENT (best risk/reward window)
- Expected duration in stage: 12-24 months
- Next stage catalyst: 40%+ awareness, corporate adoption, category definition solidifies

### Example Signals

**Positive (moving to Mainstream):**
- Major corporation announces enterprise adoption
- Category definition emerging (analyst reports published)
- IPO or major acquisition in space
- Retail availability widespread

**Negative (stalling):**
- Growth rate declining (was 5x/mo, now 1.5x/mo)
- Negative media narratives emerging
- Early adopter churn visible
- Funding rounds down/flat

## STAGE 3: MAINSTREAM (40-70% awareness)

**"Peak visibility"**

### Key Indicators

✅ **Google Trends:**
- Peak plateau (search volume stabilizing)
- Highest absolute volume
- Related queries include comparisons and "best [X]" searches

✅ **Social/Community:**
- Reddit: Front page of r/all regularly
- Twitter: Daily trending, meme saturation
- TikTok/Instagram: User-generated content flood
- "Everyone is talking about this"

✅ **Media Coverage:**
- Peak coverage, multiple articles/day
- Mainstream news (CNN, BBC, major newspapers)
- "Trend fatigue" articles starting to appear
- Late-night comedy mentions

✅ **Products:**
- 100+ SKUs available
- Full retail distribution (Amazon, Target, Walmart)
- 20+ well-funded companies in space
- Major brands all have offerings

✅ **Geography:**
- 3+ regions, widespread adoption
- Tier-2 cities showing adoption
- Global awareness (even if not adopted everywhere)

✅ **Customers:**
- Late majority adopting
- Your parents/grandparents aware of it
- Corporate adoption widespread
- Government/education sectors involved

### Strategic Implications

**Opportunity:**
- Large TAM, scale plays work
- Operational efficiency becomes key
- Ecosystem building (platforms, APIs, integrations)
- Adjacent market expansion

**Risk:**
- Commoditization beginning
- Margin compression (competition intense)
- Crowded landscape, hard to differentiate
- Peak may have passed

**Investment Approach:**
- Differentiation through execution (not concept)
- Focus on defensibility (network effects, switching costs)
- Operational excellence required
- Consider adjacencies vs. core

**Timing:**
- Entry point: LATE (only if clear differentiation)
- Expected duration in stage: 12-36 months
- Next stage catalyst: Market consolidation, innovation slowing, substitutes emerging

### Example Signals

**Positive (moving to Mature):**
- M&A consolidation beginning
- Category definitions solidified (ISO standards, regulatory frameworks)
- Public companies dominating
- Innovation increasingly incremental

**Negative (moving to Declining):**
- Negative sentiment spreading
- Substitutes gaining mindshare
- Major players exiting
- Usage metrics declining

## STAGE 4: MATURE (70%+ awareness)

**"Established category"**

### Key Indicators

✅ **Google Trends:**
- Flat or slight decline from peak
- Stable baseline established
- Searches more about specific brands than category

✅ **Social/Community:**
- Discussion shifts to complaints, comparisons, nostalgia
- Less excitement, more utility focus
- "Remember when X was new?" posts

✅ **Media Coverage:**
- Low frequency coverage (only major news)
- "State of the industry" annual pieces
- More critical/analytical vs. breathless hype

✅ **Products:**
- Consolidation visible (fewer brands, more market share concentration)
- Private label/generic versions common
- Feature parity across offerings
- Price competition primary differentiator

✅ **Geography:**
- Global ubiquity in addressable markets
- Penetration in developing markets
- No new regions to expand into

✅ **Customers:**
- Laggards finally adopting (or never will)
- Corporate usage ubiquitous, part of standard toolkit
- Low switching cost, high churn
- Buying on price not innovation

### Strategic Implications

**Opportunity:**
- Efficiency plays (reduce cost, improve margins)
- Adjacent market expansion (what's next?)
- Vertical specialization (custom for industries)
- Value/budget positioning

**Risk:**
- Disruption from new paradigms
- Declining margins (price competition)
- Slow growth (market penetrated)
- Capital fleeing to new trends

**Investment Approach:**
- Value optimization, cost management
- Platform plays (if have scale)
- Contrarian repositioning (find underserved niches)
- Roll-up strategies (acquire competitors)

**Timing:**
- Entry point: VALUE-FOCUSED ONLY
- Expected duration in stage: 3-10 years
- Next stage: Slow decline or disruption event

### Example Signals

**Positive (staying Mature):**
- Steady cash flows, predictable
- International expansion still possible
- Vertical specialization working
- Substitute threats manageable

**Negative (moving to Declining):**
- Major shift in underlying technology
- Regulatory changes threatening model
- Substitute rapidly gaining share
- Public perception shifting negative

## STAGE 5: DECLINING (negative momentum)

**"Fading or dying"**

### Key Indicators

✅ **Google Trends:**
- Consistent downward slope (>20% decline over 6 months)
- Related queries include "alternatives to" and "replacement for"

✅ **Social/Community:**
- "RIP [X]" and "Remember when..." posts
- Discussion nostalgic or critical
- Communities shrinking or moving elsewhere

✅ **Media Coverage:**
- Negative coverage or silence
- "Death of X" articles
- Post-mortems and retrospectives

✅ **Products:**
- Clearance sales, discontinued SKUs
- Retail shelf space shrinking
- Startups pivoting out of space
- Acqui-hires (talent, not product)

✅ **Geography:**
- Retreat from regions (NA first usually)
- "Still big in [less developed market]" dynamics

✅ **Customers:**
- Active churn, switching to alternatives
- No new customer acquisition
- Enterprise contracts not renewing
- Support inquiries about migration

### Strategic Implications

**Opportunity:**
- Contrarian plays if fundamentals solid (market overreacting)
- Acqui-hire targets (talent cheap)
- IP salvage (patents, technology)
- Underserved niche might remain

**Risk:**
- Dead-end investment (sunk cost fallacy)
- Reputational damage ("they backed the losing horse")
- Accelerating decline (network effects in reverse)
- Recovery rare (<10% probability)

**Investment Approach:**
- EXIT (unless specific contrarian thesis)
- Deep discount acquisition only (pennies on dollar)
- Asset stripping (IP, talent, customer list)
- Avoid sunk cost fallacy

**Timing:**
- Entry point: DON'T (unless contrarian genius)
- Duration in stage: 1-3 years until irrelevant
- End state: Complete obsolescence or zombie state (small legacy usage)

### Example Signals

**Rare positive (revival possible):**
- New use case discovered (unexpected)
- Regulatory shift reverses decline
- Major player makes big bet (contrarian validation)
- Substitute fails to deliver

**Negative (accelerating decline):**
- Bankruptcy filings
- Mass layoffs in sector
- Infrastructure shutting down
- No funding available at any price

## STAGE IDENTIFICATION CHECKLIST

**Quick diagnostic:**

1. **What % of target market is aware?** (survey, proxy with search volume)
   - <15%: Emerging
   - 15-40%: Accelerating
   - 40-70%: Mainstream
   - 70%+: Mature
   - Negative: Declining

2. **What's the trajectory?**
   - Flat: Emerging (or Declining)
   - Sharp up: Accelerating
   - Plateau: Mainstream
   - Gentle decline: Mature → Declining
   - Steep decline: Declining

3. **Where is it discussed?**
   - Niche communities: Emerging
   - Tech press + communities: Accelerating
   - Mainstream media: Mainstream
   - Analyst reports: Mature
   - Nowhere: Declining

4. **How many products?**
   - <10: Emerging
   - 10-100: Accelerating
   - 100+: Mainstream
   - Consolidating: Mature
   - Shrinking: Declining

5. **Who's buying?**
   - Innovators: Emerging
   - Early adopters: Accelerating
   - Early/late majority: Mainstream
   - Laggards: Mature
   - No one: Declining

| Your Profile | Best Stage | Rationale |
|--------------|-----------|-----------|
| **High risk tolerance, seeking alpha** | Emerging | First-mover advantage, highest upside |
| **Growth-oriented, strong execution** | Accelerating | Best risk/reward, validation + growth |

## COMMON MISTAKES

❌ **Confusing hype for actual stage**
- Media hype ≠ actual adoption
- Check product availability and customer data

❌ **Missing regional differences**
- Trend can be Mainstream in NA, Emerging in EU
- Always specify region with stage

❌ **Mistiming the peak**
- Many enter at Mainstream thinking it's Accelerating
- Check trajectory, not just absolute level

❌ **Ignoring velocity**
- Stage matters, but velocity through stages matters more
- Fast Emerging → Accelerating (3 months) is different from slow (24 months)

❌ **Static assessment**
- Stages change quickly (monthly reassessment needed)
- Today's Accelerating is next quarter's Mainstream

## STAGE TRANSITIONS

### Emerging → Accelerating (positive)
**Typical timeline:** 6-18 months
**Triggers:**
- Major funding round (Series B+)
- Mainstream media coverage begins
- Product availability 3x+
- Geographic expansion (1 region → 2-3)

### Accelerating → Mainstream (positive)
**Typical timeline:** 12-24 months
**Triggers:**
- Corporate adoption announced
- 40%+ awareness in surveys
- Retail distribution widespread
- Category definition solidified

### Mainstream → Mature (neutral)
**Typical timeline:** 12-36 months
**Triggers:**
- M&A consolidation
- Market penetration >70%
- Innovation slowing (incremental only)
- Price competition dominating

### Mature → Declining (negative)
**Typical timeline:** Variable (3-10 years)
**Triggers:**
- Substitute technology emerges
- Regulatory changes
- Changing user preferences
- Technological obsolescence

### Any → Declining (negative shock)
**Typical timeline:** 3-12 months
**Triggers:**
- Regulatory ban
- Security/safety crisis
- Better substitute rapidly adopted
- Major player exit

## WORKED EXAMPLES

### Example 1: Electric Vehicles (by region, Oct 2025)

| Region | Stage | Awareness | Evidence |
|--------|-------|-----------|----------|
| Norway | Mature | 80%+ | 90% of new sales electric |
| China | Mainstream | 50%+ | BYD, NIO widespread |
| California | Accelerating | 30%+ | Growing fast, not yet majority |
| US (non-CA) | Emerging/Accelerating | 15-20% | Growing but low base |
| India | Emerging | <10% | Early stage, mostly 2-wheelers |
| Africa | Pre-emerging | <5% | Limited signals |

**Key insight:** Same trend, vastly different stages by region

### Example 2: AI Coding Assistants (Oct 2025)

**Stage:** Accelerating (25-30% awareness among developers)

**Evidence:**
- Google Trends: 4x growth in 6 months
- GitHub Copilot: Millions of users (GitHub reported)
- Media: TechCrunch, The Information, Bloomberg regular coverage
- Products: 10+ tools (Copilot, Cursor, Replit, Codeium, Tabnine, etc.)
- Geography: NA + EU + India showing signals
- Customers: Early majority developers adopting

**Trajectory:** Moving toward Mainstream (12-18 month estimate)

**Catalysts to watch:**
- Enterprise rollout announcements from F500
- IDE bundling (VS Code default integration)
- Developer bootcamp curriculum changes

### Example 3: Clubhouse (Apr 2021 vs. Oct 2025)

**Apr 2021:** Accelerating
- Hype peak, mainstream media coverage
- Celebrity usage, high download rates
- BUT: Product availability limited (iOS only, invite-only)

**Oct 2025:** Declining
- Minimal media mentions
- App ranking fallen
- Substitutes (Twitter Spaces, Discord stages) dominant
- Talent departed to other companies

**Key insight:** Hype ≠ sustainable stage, need product and adoption to validate
