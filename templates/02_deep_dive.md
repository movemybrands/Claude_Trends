# Template 2: Deep Dive (Single Trend)

**When to use:** Evaluating a specific investment, preparing for strategic decision, due diligence on a trend

---

## Prompt Template

```
DEEP DIVE REQUEST

Using my [Trend Intelligence Methodology v1.0], analyze this specific trend in depth:

**Trend:** [Name of trend - be specific]

**Analysis Framework:**
1. Full confidence scoring (show all 4 dimensions with detailed calculations)
2. Lifecycle stage assessment with supporting evidence
3. Regional breakdown (which regions, what stage in each, timing patterns)
4. Competitive landscape (who's involved, positioning, market shares if available)
5. Monetization analysis (business models, unit economics if available, revenue potential)
6. Risk factors (what could kill this trend, probability assessment)
7. Opportunity analysis (addressable market, timing windows, strategic angles)
8. Action plan (specific next steps for [your context: operator/investor/analyst])

**Data Requirements:**
- Use web_search for latest signals (past [30/60/90] days)
- Include 10+ cited sources (aim for mix of Tier-1 and Tier-2)
- Target: 1,500-2,000 words total

**Special Focus:** [Any specific angle you want emphasized]
Examples:
- Technical feasibility assessment
- Competitive moat analysis
- Market sizing and TAM estimation
- Go-to-market strategy recommendations
- Regulatory/policy implications
- Customer adoption barriers

**Output Format:** Long-form analysis with clear section headers, not newsletter style
```

---

## Example Usage

```
DEEP DIVE REQUEST

Using my [Trend Intelligence Methodology v1.0], analyze this specific trend in depth:

**Trend:** AI-powered code review tools replacing manual code review processes in enterprise software development

**Analysis Framework:**
1. Full confidence scoring (show all 4 dimensions with detailed calculations)
2. Lifecycle stage assessment with supporting evidence
3. Regional breakdown (NA/EU/APAC focus)
4. Competitive landscape (identify key players, positioning, recent funding)
5. Monetization analysis (pricing models, unit economics, typical deal sizes)
6. Risk factors (technical limitations, adoption barriers, competitive threats)
7. Opportunity analysis (market size, growth rate, timing windows)
8. Action plan (build/buy/partner recommendations for a Series B SaaS company)

**Data Requirements:**
- Use web_search for signals from past 60 days
- Include 12+ sources with emphasis on Tier-1 data
- Target: 2,000 words

**Special Focus:**
- Technical maturity assessment (is the technology production-ready?)
- Integration complexity with existing developer workflows
- ROI case studies from early adopters
- Competitive differentiation factors

**Output Format:** Long-form strategic brief
```

## Expected Output Structure

### Executive Summary
- Trend name and core thesis
- Confidence score with one-paragraph justification
- Stage and trajectory
- Top 3 opportunities and top 3 risks
- Clear recommendation (invest/pass/monitor)

### 1. Confidence Score Analysis
**Dimension A: Source Quality (XX/100)**
- List of sources used (with tier classifications)
- Why this score (e.g., "2 Tier-1 sources: Gartner report + Bloomberg article with primary data")

**Dimension B: Signal Strength (XX/100)**
- Quantitative metrics: [specific data points]
- Multi-platform presence: [list platforms]
- Geographic spread: [regions with evidence]
- Persistence: [time series data]

**Dimension C: Coherence (XX/100)**
- Narrative: [clear story of why now, who cares, what changed]
- Catalyst: [specific unlock moment]
- Monetization: [revenue models and validation]
- Conflicts: [any contradictory data and how reconciled]

**Dimension D: Timing (XX/100)**
- Data freshness assessment
- Justification for score

**Final Score:** (A+B+C+D)/4 = XX% 🟢/🟡/🔴

### 2. Lifecycle Stage Assessment
**Current Stage:** [Emerging/Accelerating/Mainstream/Mature/Declining]

**Evidence:**
- Awareness indicators: [search trends, media mentions, community discussion volume]
- Adoption indicators: [customer counts, product availability, geographic spread]
- Stage-specific characteristics: [map to framework definitions]

**Trajectory:**
- Where will this be in 6 months? 12 months?
- What needs to happen for stage progression?
- What could cause regression?

### 3. Regional Analysis

| Region | Stage | Confidence | Timeline | Key Players |
|--------|-------|------------|----------|-------------|
| NA | [Stage] | XX% | [When started] | [Companies] |
| EU | [Stage] | XX% | [When started] | [Companies] |
| APAC | [Stage] | XX% | [When started] | [Companies] |

**Migration Patterns:**
- Where did trend originate?
- How is it spreading geographically?
- Regional adaptations or variations
- Next regions likely to see uptake

### 4. Competitive Landscape

**Market Structure:**
- Number of players (fragmented vs. consolidated)
- Market leaders and their positioning
- Recent entrants and their angles
- Adjacent players likely to enter

**Key Players Analysis:**

| Company | Stage | Positioning | Recent Activity | Strengths/Weaknesses |
|---------|-------|-------------|-----------------|----------------------|
| [Name] | [Startup/Growth/Public] | [Differentiation] | [Funding/Product/Partnerships] | [Analysis] |

**Competitive Dynamics:**
- Who's winning and why?
- What are the differentiation vectors?
- Are there network effects or winner-take-all dynamics?
- M&A activity or consolidation signals?

### 5. Monetization Analysis

**Business Models Observed:**
- [Model 1]: Description, examples, typical pricing
- [Model 2]: Description, examples, typical pricing

**Unit Economics (where available):**
- Customer acquisition cost
- Average deal size / ACV
- Payback period
- Retention / churn rates
- Gross margins

**Revenue Potential:**
- TAM estimation (with methodology)
- Current market size
- Growth rate projections (with justification)
- Addressable customer segments

**Validation Points:**
- Companies achieving $XXM+ ARR
- Public pricing data
- Funding amounts as proxy for revenue expectations

### 6. Risk Assessment

**Technical Risks:**
- Technology maturity concerns
- Scalability limitations
- Dependency on underlying platforms

**Market Risks:**
- Adoption barriers (cost, change management, inertia)
- Competitive threats (incumbents, well-funded startups)
- Market timing (too early vs. too late)

**Macro Risks:**
- Regulatory or policy changes
- Economic sensitivity
- Dependency on other trends

**Kill Scenarios:**
Specific conditions that would invalidate the trend:
1. [Scenario 1]: Probability XX%, Impact [High/Med/Low]
2. [Scenario 2]: Probability XX%, Impact [High/Med/Low]

### 7. Opportunity Analysis

**Market Opportunity:**
- Addressable market size: $XXM - $XXB
- Growth trajectory: XX% CAGR
- Time to mainstream: [X months/years]

**Strategic Windows:**
- Best entry point: [Now / 6mo / 12mo / wait]
- Window duration: [How long before commoditization]
- Key timing factors: [What's driving urgency or patience]

**Attack Vectors:**
- Approach 1: [Description, pros/cons]
- Approach 2: [Description, pros/cons]
- Recommended approach: [Rationale]

**Success Factors:**
- What would a winner need to have?
- What are the common failure modes?
- What resources/capabilities required?

### 8. Action Plan

**For [Operators/Investors/Analysts]:**

**Immediate (Next 30 days):**
1. [Specific action with owner and deliverable]
2. [Specific action with owner and deliverable]

**Short-term (1-3 months):**
1. [Milestone or decision point]
2. [Resource allocation or partnership development]

**Medium-term (3-6 months):**
1. [Scale decision or pivot point]
2. [Success criteria or kill criteria]

**Key Decisions:**
- Build vs. Buy vs. Partner: [Recommendation with rationale]
- Resource commitment: [Dollar amount / headcount / time]
- Success metrics: [What would confirm this is working?]
- Exit criteria: [What would trigger a stop/pivot?]

### 9. Validation Roadmap

**Questions to answer:**
1. [Critical unknown]: How to validate: [Method]
2. [Critical unknown]: How to validate: [Method]

**Data to track:**
- Weekly: [Metric 1, Metric 2]
- Monthly: [Metric 3, Metric 4]
- Quarterly: [Stage assessment, confidence recalc]

**Decision triggers:**
- If [X happens] → [Action]
- If [Y happens] → [Action]

---

## Output Quality Checklist

Before accepting the output, verify:
- [ ] All 4 confidence dimensions calculated with justification
- [ ] 10+ sources cited with dates
- [ ] Competitive landscape includes 5+ specific companies
- [ ] At least one monetization model described with examples
- [ ] 3+ specific risks identified with probability estimates
- [ ] Action plan has specific next steps (not vague "monitor")
- [ ] Validation roadmap includes measurable triggers
- [ ] Total length 1,500-2,000 words

---

## Customization Options

**Investor focus:**
- Add section on comparable companies and valuations
- Include exit landscape analysis
- Emphasize capital efficiency and time to ROI

**Operator focus:**
- Add section on build roadmap and resources required
- Include go-to-market strategy specifics
- Emphasize competitive differentiation and moats

**Analyst focus:**
- Add section on related trends and dependencies
- Include scenario planning (bull/base/bear cases)
- Emphasize data quality and confidence intervals
