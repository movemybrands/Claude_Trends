# TREND INTELLIGENCE METHODOLOGY
**Version:** 1.0
**Last Updated:** October 23, 2025
**Owner:** Claude Trends Framework
**Purpose:** Evidence-based framework for analyzing emerging commercial, technological, and cultural trends

---

## CORE PHILOSOPHY

Trends are hypotheses requiring continuous validation, not facts. This methodology prioritizes:
- Early-stage pattern recognition (trends at <20% mainstream awareness)
- Multi-regional perspective (NA, EU, APAC primary; LATAM, MEA secondary)
- Confidence-weighted analysis (no speculation without evidence)
- Portfolio management approach (track, update, kill/revive signals)

---

## SOURCE RELIABILITY FRAMEWORK

### Tier 1: High Reliability (Primary data, hard metrics, official sources)
**Use for:** Confidence scores 80%+
**Examples:**
- Google Trends data (search volume, related queries)
- E-commerce velocity (Amazon/Etsy BSR changes, app store rankings)
- Financial data (funding rounds, revenue disclosures, SEC filings)
- Regulatory filings, patent applications
- Major industry reports (Gartner, Forrester, McKinsey, BCG)

**Validation criteria:** Published methodology, transparent data sources, replicable

---

### Tier 2: Moderate Reliability (Curated secondary sources)
**Use for:** Confidence scores 60-80%
**Examples:**
- Established industry newsletters (Morning Brew, The Information, Axios, Stratechery)
- Trade show announcements with vendor participation data
- LinkedIn groups with 10K+ active members showing sustained discussion
- Creator/influencer content with verifiable engagement data (views, shares, comments)

**Validation criteria:** Editorial oversight, named authors, track record of accuracy

---

### Tier 3: Exploratory (Early weak signals)
**Use for:** Confidence scores <60% OR as supporting evidence
**Examples:**
- Niche subreddit discussions (if >100 upvotes OR 50+ substantive comments)
- Discord/Slack community mentions (if corroborated across 2+ communities)
- Academic preprints and grant announcements
- Micro-influencer content (if spreading cross-platform)
- TikTok/Instagram trends (if migration beyond origin platform visible)

**Validation criteria:** Corroboration required, note sample size limitations

---

### Auto-Discard
**Never use for confidence scoring:**
- Single-source rumors without independent verification
- Anonymous claims (unless whistleblower with documentation)
- Press releases without independent media coverage
- Personal blogs without established track record
- Marketing content disguised as editorial

## TREND LIFECYCLE STAGES

### Stage 1: EMERGING (0-15% awareness)
**Characteristics:**
- Primarily niche community discussions
- Early adopter purchases only
- Search interest beginning to curve upward
- Limited geographic spread (1-2 regions typically)

**Opportunity:** First-mover advantage, low competition, high optionality
**Risk:** May never break out, limited data for validation, high failure rate

**Investment approach:** Small bets, rapid experimentation, low commitment

---

### Stage 2: ACCELERATING (15-40% awareness)
**Characteristics:**
- Multi-platform mentions increasing week-over-week
- Mainstream media starting to cover
- Product availability expanding (more SKUs, channels, vendors)
- Geographic expansion visible

**Opportunity:** Strong growth phase, validation occurring, still early enough
**Risk:** Competition entering rapidly, window closing, hype cycle beginning

**Investment approach:** Scale validated experiments, commit resources, move quickly

---

### Stage 3: MAINSTREAM (40-70% awareness)
**Characteristics:**
- Peak media coverage
- Saturation in early-adopter segments
- Corporate adoption or major brand launches
- Established category definitions

**Opportunity:** Scale plays, operational efficiency, ecosystem building
**Risk:** Commoditization, margin compression, crowded competitive landscape

**Investment approach:** Differentiation through execution, defensibility focus

---

### Stage 4: MATURE (70%+ awareness)
**Characteristics:**
- Ubiquitous in target market
- Innovation increasingly incremental
- Defensive positioning by incumbents
- Consolidation phase (M&A activity)

**Opportunity:** Efficiency plays, adjacent market expansion, vertical specialization
**Risk:** Disruption from new paradigms, declining margins, slow growth

**Investment approach:** Value optimization, platform plays, contrarian repositioning

---

### Stage 5: DECLINING (negative momentum)
**Characteristics:**
- Substitutes emerging and gaining traction
- Media narrative turns negative or disappears
- Usage/search metrics falling consistently
- Capital fleeing category

**Opportunity:** Contrarian plays if fundamentals remain solid, acqui-hire targets
**Risk:** Dead-end investment, sunk cost fallacy, reputational damage

**Investment approach:** Exit or deep discount acquisition, salvage IP/talent

## CONFIDENCE SCORING SYSTEM (0-100 scale)

### Calculation Method
Average scores across 4 dimensions, round to nearest integer.

---

### DIMENSION A: Source Quality (0-100 points)

| Sources | Points | Use Case |
|---------|--------|----------|
| 3+ Tier-1 sources | 100 | High-confidence calls |
| 2 Tier-1 OR 4+ Tier-2 | 80 | Strong signals |
| 1 Tier-1 OR 3 Tier-2 | 60 | Medium confidence |
| Mostly Tier-3 but corroborated | 40 | Early exploration |
| Single source or uncorroborated | 20 | Weak signal only |

**Scoring notes:**
- Mix matters: 1 Tier-1 + 2 Tier-2 = 70 pts (interpolate between bands)
- Independence matters: Same data repackaged by multiple outlets = single source
- Recency matters: Tier-1 source from 6 months ago downgrades to Tier-2 equivalent

---

### DIMENSION B: Signal Strength (0-100 points)

Build score additively:

| Signal Type | Points | Validation |
|-------------|--------|------------|
| Quantitative metrics available | +40 | Search volume, sales rank, engagement counts |
| Multi-platform presence | +30 | 3+ platforms (define: social, news, commerce, communities) |
| Geographic spread | +20 | 2+ regions with independent validation |
| Persistence over time | +10 | 3+ consecutive data points (days/weeks depending on trend) |

**Examples:**
- Google Trends + Reddit + TikTok = 3 platforms (+30)
- Data from NA and EU = 2 regions (+20)
- Search data from Mon/Wed/Fri last week = persistence (+10)
- No hard metrics available = 0 for quantitative

**Maximum possible:** 100 points (all criteria met)

---

### DIMENSION C: Coherence (0-100 points)

Build score additively:

| Element | Points | Validation |
|---------|--------|------------|
| Clear narrative | +40 | Can explain: why now, who cares, what changed |
| Identified catalyst | +30 | Specific trigger: tech breakthrough, regulatory shift, cultural moment |
| Monetization pathway visible | +20 | Clear business model or revenue mechanism |
| No major conflicting data | +10 | Sources align, no unexplained contradictions |

**Narrative quality check:**
- Can you explain the trend to a smart 12-year-old? (+40)
- Can you point to a specific "unlock" moment? (+30)
- Can you describe how someone makes money from this? (+20)
- Do all sources tell consistent story? (+10)

**Red flags that reduce score:**
- Vague buzzwords without specifics: -10
- Conflicting data not reconciled: -10
- Circular reasoning (trend exists because people talk about trend): -20

---

### DIMENSION D: Timing (0-100 points)

| Data Age | Points | Rationale |
|----------|--------|-----------|
| <48 hours | 100 | Real-time signal |
| 2-7 days | 80 | Very recent |
| 1-4 weeks | 60 | Recent enough |
| 1-3 months | 40 | Aging, needs refresh |
| >3 months | 20 | Stale, low value |

**Special cases:**
- For slow-moving trends (B2B SaaS adoption), add 20 pts per band (e.g., 1-3 months = 60)
- For fast-moving trends (viral social content), subtract 20 pts per band (e.g., 2-7 days = 60)
- If historical trend with recent validation, use recent validation date

---

### FINAL CONFIDENCE SCORE

**Formula:** (A + B + C + D) / 4, rounded to nearest integer

**Action Thresholds:**

| Score | Color | Signal | Action |
|-------|-------|--------|--------|
| 80-100 | 🟢 Green | High confidence | **ACT**: Invest, build, allocate resources, commit team |
| 60-79 | 🟡 Yellow | Medium confidence | **TEST**: Pilot, small bet, defined exit criteria, monitor closely |
| 0-59 | 🔴 Red | Low confidence | **WATCH**: No resource commitment, track for updates only |

**Confidence boundaries:**
- Never assign 80%+ without ≥2 Tier-1 sources (Exception: single source is primary research you conducted)
- Yellow range (60-79) should have clear test parameters: "If X happens in Y timeframe, upgrade to green"
- Red range (<60) acceptable for "weak signal" exploration but must be labeled as such

## TREND PORTFOLIO MANAGEMENT

### Portfolio Rules
- **Maximum active trends:** 15 (focus > coverage)
- **Minimum confidence for active tracking:** 50% (below this = archive)
- **Target distribution:** 3-5 Green, 5-8 Yellow, 2-4 Red
- **Review cadence:** Weekly for actives, monthly for portfolio composition

---

### Kill Signals (Stop tracking when)
1. Confidence drops ≥20 points in 30 days with no recovery trajectory
2. Fails validation on 3+ attempted confirmations (tried to find new data, couldn't)
3. Strong negative catalyst: regulatory ban, major player exit, better substitute emerges
4. Stage regresses without new data (e.g., Accelerating → Emerging suggests measurement error)

**Kill process:**
- Move to "Archived" section with kill date and reason
- Set reminder for 90-day review (potential revival check)

---

### Revival Signals (Reactivate when)
1. Confidence recovers ≥15 points from trough within 60 days
2. New Tier-1 source validates with fresh primary data
3. Expansion to new region or demographic segment
4. Paradigm shift: new use case discovered, unexpected catalyst

**Revival process:**
- Recalculate confidence from scratch (don't just carry forward old score)
- Note what changed in "Revival Catalyst" field
- Reset "Days Tracked" counter

---

### Weekly Review Checklist
- [ ] Update confidence scores for all active trends (if new data available)
- [ ] Check for kill signals
- [ ] Identify top 3 confidence gainers/losers
- [ ] Add 0-2 new trends (if meet 50% threshold)
- [ ] Prune trends below 40% confidence for 2+ consecutive weeks

---

### Monthly Deep Dive
- [ ] Stage progression analysis (are trends moving through lifecycle as expected?)
- [ ] ROI on past calls (which 80%+ confidence trends panned out? Which didn't?)
- [ ] Source quality audit (which sources have been most predictive?)
- [ ] Regional pattern analysis (any diffusion patterns emerging?)
- [ ] Vertical correlation (trends clustering in specific sectors?)

## QUALITY CONTROL STANDARDS

### Before Publishing Any Analysis

**Source Check:**
- [ ] All Tier-1 claims have ≥2 independent sources cited
- [ ] Dates included for all data points
- [ ] No broken links or inaccessible sources
- [ ] Press releases flagged as such (not treated as independent validation)

**Confidence Integrity:**
- [ ] Score calculated using all 4 dimensions
- [ ] No 80%+ scores without ≥2 Tier-1 sources
- [ ] Yellow/Red ranges have clear upgrade criteria
- [ ] Uncertainty explicitly noted where present

**Stage Assignment:**
- [ ] Based on observable metrics (search volume, media mentions, product availability)
- [ ] Matches awareness percentage bands
- [ ] Uncertainty flagged if borderline between stages

**Regional Data:**
- [ ] Sparse data explicitly acknowledged
- [ ] No regional claims without regional sources
- [ ] Migration patterns based on timing data, not assumptions

**Inference Separation:**
- [ ] Clear distinction between "what we know" and "what we infer"
- [ ] Speculation labeled as such
- [ ] Causation vs. correlation clarified

## LIMITATIONS & DISCLOSURES

**Always include in reports:**

1. **Prediction Disclaimer:**
"Confidence scores are analytical frameworks for evaluating evidence strength, not predictions of future outcomes. Early-stage trends have 40-60% failure rates even with strong signals."

2. **Data Constraints:**
"Analysis based on [describe sources: web search, provided data, etc.]. Real-time platform metrics (social engagement, app rankings) not directly accessible; assessments based on secondary reporting."

3. **Regional Coverage:**
"Regional analysis limited by [language barriers, source availability, market access]. [Specify which regions have stronger/weaker coverage]."

4. **Temporal Limitations:**
"Trend intelligence is time-sensitive. Confidence scores degrade as data ages. Reassessment recommended every [7-30 days depending on trend velocity]."
