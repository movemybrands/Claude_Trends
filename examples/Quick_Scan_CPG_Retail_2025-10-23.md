3. Unilever, P&G, Nestlé investing $2B+ combined in packaging redesign 2024-2026; Coca-Cola 25% recycled content globally by 2025 target, 100% recyclable packaging by 2025 (Company sustainability reports, 2024-2025) - Tier 2

**Action:** WATCH for 30 days - If CPG manufacturer, audit packaging compliance against EU PPWR and California SB 54 (strictest standards) to identify gaps. Budget 5-10% of COGS for redesign over 24 months if non-compliant. If retailer, engage suppliers on compliance timelines to avoid supply disruption. If packaging supplier, R&D shift to mono-materials (easier recycling) and bio-based alternatives.

**Confidence Breakdown:** Source Quality: 80 (EU regulation Tier-1 + state laws Tier-1 + company reports Tier-2), Signal Strength: 75 (quantitative: 12 U.S. states with EPR, 25% reduction targets, $2B+ investment + multi-region regulatory forcing function + 3-year window), Coherence: 70 (narrative clear: regulation → redesign, but implementation timelines uncertain, monetization: compliance cost vs. brand risk), Timing: 65 (EU PPWR April 2024 = 18 months old, CA SB 54 Jan 2023 = older, but ongoing).
